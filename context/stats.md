# 📊 Statistics Dashboard

The Statistics Dashboard helps users monitor their study habits, productivity, and quiz performance over time.

## 📌 Summary Cards

Quick overview of the user's progress.

- ⏱️ **Total Study Time** – Total hours spent in focus sessions.
- ☕ **Total Break Time** – Total hours spent on short and long breaks.
- 🍅 **Completed Pomodoro Sessions** – Number of successfully completed focus sessions.
- 📚 **Total Quizzes Taken** – Total quizzes completed.
- ❓ **Total Questions Answered** – Total quiz questions attempted.
- 🎯 **Average Quiz Score** – Average score across all quizzes.
- 🔥 **Current Study Streak** – Consecutive days with at least one completed study session.

---

## 📈 Charts & Analytics

### 📅 Study Time Per Day
**Visual:** Bar Chart

Displays the total study time for each day within the selected time period, allowing users to monitor their daily consistency.

---

### 📆 Study Time by Day of the Week
**Visual:** Bar Chart

Shows the accumulated study time for each day of the week (Monday–Sunday). This helps users identify which days they are most productive.

---

### 📈 Weekly Study Trend
**Visual:** Line Chart

Shows the total study hours for each week, making it easier to observe long-term productivity trends.

---

### 📊 Monthly Study Trend
**Visual:** Line Chart

Displays the total study hours for each month to help users track their study habits over a longer period.

---

### ☕ Study Time vs Break Time
**Visual:** Doughnut Chart

Compares the total time spent studying against the total time spent on breaks, providing an overview of time allocation.

---

### 📚 Study Time by Subject
**Visual:** Pie Chart / Doughnut Chart

Illustrates how the user's study time is distributed across different subjects, helping identify where most study time is invested.

---

### 🎯 Quiz Accuracy
**Visual:** Doughnut Chart

Displays the percentage of correctly answered and incorrectly answered quiz questions.

---

### 📖 Quiz Performance by Subject
**Visual:** Horizontal Bar Chart

Shows the average quiz score for each subject, allowing users to identify strengths and subjects that may require additional review.
